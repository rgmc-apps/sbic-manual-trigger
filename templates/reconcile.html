Compares BigQuery's raw Document AI-parsed PO records against MSSQL, Business Central, and the SO-import Buffer. BigQuery is always the source of truth — MSSQL and BC are checked against it, never the other way around.
  • An exact PO ref search also checks CustomerPOUL for refs BigQuery never got at all (e.g. legacy-app entries) — flagged "Not in BigQuery."
  • Company filters results to one BC company, matched against BigQuery's free-text company name field — recognizes known variants/abbreviations of a company's name (e.g. SBIC also matches "Suncoast", "Suncoast Brands International Corp.", "Suncoast Brands Intl Corp."), not just an exact string.
  • The Buffer badge shows whether a PO is already sitting in the Firestore buffer, waiting on reconciliation or a retry — not necessarily bad, but it means Quick Align All will merge in missing lines and trigger Reprocess Buffer rather than inserting it fresh. Expand Show detail lines on a buffered PO to see which individual items are already there.
  • ⚡ Quick Align All is the one button that fixes every result shown, using the same existing, already-vetted tools this page always used — not yet in BC → inserts into MSSQL + buffers it, then one Reprocess Buffer trigger (batched across every company touched) covers every PO that needed it; in BC but missing lines → one Sync from Cloud SQL trigger (also batched across companies) covers every PO missing lines. It never writes to BC directly.
  • Reprocessing/syncing happens in the background — it is not instant. Re-run the search later, or check the Buffer/History tabs, to confirm each PO actually resolved in BC; a click here is a trigger, not a guarantee, so treat Business Central itself as the final check.
  • 📋 Generate Shareable Report acts on every result currently shown — gives a link anyone can open to see each PO's status, has its own ⚡ Quick Align All button (needs their own name/email first), a direct link to the Buffer tab per company, and a print-to-PDF option.
  • Use Group by above the results to bucket them by what's missing — everything absent from both MSSQL and BC, from just one of the two, status-unknown (a live check failed — re-run the search), and fully aligned — instead of scanning badges on a flat list.

Searches Business Central directly for Sales Orders matching the filters above, then compares each order's current lines against the original Document AI-extracted PO lines (BigQuery/Cloud SQL) to find items that never made it onto the BC order.
